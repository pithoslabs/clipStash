# Code of Conduct

ClipStash is a source-available project for people who want a small, private, native clipboard manager for macOS. Keep participation respectful, technical, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions focused on the issue, pull request, or design decision.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Assume privacy and security concerns are worth taking seriously.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Publishing private information without permission
- Posting real secrets, passwords, tokens, or private clipboard contents
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately at info@pithoslabs.org. Security vulnerabilities should also be reported through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when needed to protect the project.
